How do you calculate YouTube RPM?

Divide YouTube revenue by views for the same period and multiply by 1,000. For example, $400 from 100,000 views gives $4 RPM. To forecast revenue, multiply expected views by RPM and divide by 1,000. To find the views needed for a target, divide your target by RPM, multiply by 1,000 and round up.

Studio RPM already reflects YouTube’s revenue share. It can include ads, YouTube Premium, memberships and certain fan payments. Use engaged views for Shorts RPM, and keep Shorts and watch-page video inputs separate. YouTube Help on RPM and CPM

Is YouTube RPM the same as CPM?

CPM is the advertiser’s cost per 1,000 ad impressions. RPM measures creator revenue per 1,000 views after revenue sharing, including views that did not show an ad. Use the same format and reporting period for your inputs. Budget business expenses, taxes and brand deals separately.
